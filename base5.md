In a global collapse, Bangladesh’s extreme population density and humidity make **mobility** and **food preservation** your two biggest hurdles. To gain an advantage, you must transition from "buying ready-made" to "owning the means of production and transport."

Here is your survival and advantage tactic for Transport and Food, specifically tailored for the Bangladesh landscape.

---

### 1. Transport: Mobility Without Fuel
In a war or collapse, the government will likely seize fuel for the military. You need a way to move your family and goods that doesn't rely on the grid.

*   **The "Solarized" Rickshaw/Van (High Advantage):**
    *   **Buy Now:** A standard cycle-van (used for hauling goods) or a rickshaw.
    *   **The Hack:** Convert it with a **48V 1000W BLDC Motor Kit** (available in Dhaka’s Nawabpur or Stadium Market for ~12,000–15,000 BDT) and mount **two 200W-300W rigid solar panels** as the roof.
    *   **Advantage:** While others are stuck walking, you have a self-charging vehicle that can carry 3-4 people plus 200kg of supplies indefinitely.
*   **The Inflatable or Small Wooden Boat:**
    *   **Why:** Bangladesh is a delta. If roads are blocked or bombed, the rivers and canals are your "highways." 
    *   **Buy/Save:** A small 2-stroke outboard motor (like a "China Engine") and a manual for **Wood Gasification**.
    *   **The Advanced Hack:** Download a guide on **Wood Gasifiers**. In a total fuel collapse, you can run a modified gasoline engine on "smoke" from burning wood scraps or dried cow dung—abundant in BD.
*   **Bicycle Spare Parts:**
    *   **Buy Now:** 10 extra tubes, 5 tires, and several chains for every bike you own. In a collapse, these will become "gold." A bike with a trailer is the most reliable transport in the world.

### 2. Food Strategy: The 3-Tier System
In Bangladesh, humidity will rot your food faster than in the West. You must use "Active" and "Passive" storage.

#### Tier 1: Long-Term Grains (Store Now)
*   **The "Paddy" Secret:** If possible, store **Unmilled Rice (Paddy/Dhaan)** instead of white rice. The husk protects the grain from insects and moisture for much longer.
*   **Airtight & Natural Repellents:** Use 20-liter food-grade plastic buckets or **"Matka" (clay pots)** sealed with wax. 
    *   **Advantage Hack:** Mix in a handful of **Dried Neem Leaves** and **Dried Red Chilies**. This is a traditional Bangladeshi method that prevents weevil (surpoka) infestations for years without chemicals.
*   **Mustard Oil (Shorsher Tel):** Buy in bulk. It is high-calorie, doesn't go rancid as fast as soybean oil, and acts as a preservative for pickling.

#### Tier 2: Urban Production (Grow Now)
*   **Vertical Rooftop Gardening:** If you are in Dhaka/Chittagong, your roof is your life support. 
    *   **Focus on Calories:** Grow Sweet Potato (Mishti Alu), Moringa (Sojne), and Taro (Kachu). These are "survival plants"—they are hard to kill, nutrient-dense, and almost every part (leaves and roots) is edible.
*   **Seed Saving:** Buy "Open-Pollinated" seeds from **BADC (Bangladesh Agricultural Development Corp)**. Do not buy "Hybrid" seeds; you cannot replant their seeds next year. 

#### Tier 3: Wild Edibles & Preservation (The Knowledge Advantage)
Download the list of **"Wild Shaks" (Leafy Greens)** common in the BD plains that most city people think are weeds:
*   **Kalmi Shak:** Grows in any water body; highly nutritious.
*   **Helancha Shak:** Found near ponds; medicinal and edible.
*   **Thankuni:** Grows on the ground; the ultimate survival medicine for stomach issues (dysentery), which will be common in a collapse.
*   **Preservation (Shutki & Shidol):** Learn to dry fish (Shutki) and ferment it (Shidol). Dried fish provides high-protein storage that requires zero refrigeration.

### 3. Water: The Hidden Requirement
Food is useless without water. In BD, the "advantage" is being able to drink from a pond safely.
*   **Buy Alum (Fitkari) in Bulk:** It costs pennies in local bazaars. A small piece of Fitkari swirled in a bucket of muddy pond water will settle all dirt and pathogens to the bottom in minutes (Coagulation). 
*   **The Bio-Sand Filter:** Save a guide on how to build one using local sand, gravel, and a plastic barrel. This will provide your family with 50 liters of clean water daily with zero power.
