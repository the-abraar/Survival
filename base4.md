This is a massive amount of data. To prepare effectively in Bangladesh, you need a **1TB External SSD or several 256GB MicroSD cards**. Do not use cheap "no-name" USB drives; they will fail.

Here are the direct sources and download paths for your survival digital library.

### 1. The Offline Encyclopedia (Kiwix)
Kiwix compresses entire websites into `.zim` files. You need the **Kiwix Desktop** or **Kiwix Android** app first.

*   **Software:** [Download Kiwix Reader](https://www.kiwix.org/en/download/)
*   **The Content (ZIM Files):** [Kiwix Library Archive](https://library.kiwix.org/)
    *   *Search for these specific files in the library:*
    *   **Wikipedia English (Full):** ~100GB (Everything: History, Science, Tech).
    *   **WikiMed:** ~5GB (Essential medical knowledge/surgeries).
    *   **StackExchange (Sustainability):** ~1GB (How to build solar, water filters, etc.).
    *   **Wikivoyage:** ~1GB (Geography and maps).
    *   **TED Talks (Health):** ~5GB (Video guides on mental/physical health).

### 2. Survival & Technical Manuals (PDFs)
These are "Gold Standard" documents. Download them individually and organize them into folders.

*   **Hesperian Health Guides (Life-Saving):**
    *   [Where There Is No Doctor](https://hesperian.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/en_wtnd_2022/en_wtnd_2022_full.pdf)
    *   [Where There Is No Dentist](https://hesperian.org/wp-content/uploads/pdf/en_wtndent_2022/en_wtndent_2022_full.pdf)
*   **Military Survival Manuals:**
    *   [US Army Survival Manual FM 21-76](https://ia800203.us.archive.org/33/items/usa-army-survival-manual-21-76/usa-army-survival-manual-21-76.pdf)
*   **The "Survival Library" Repository:**
    *   [Survivalistboards.com Files](https://www.survivalistboards.com/downloads.php) (Requires account).
    *   [Appropedia.org](https://www.appropedia.org/): Focuses on "Appropriate Technology" (building things in developing countries). Highly relevant for BD.

### 3. Local LLMs (The "Offline AI Brain")
In a collapse, you can't "Google" a question. An offline AI can answer technical questions using only your laptop's battery.

*   **The Software:** [Download Ollama](https://ollama.com/) (Simplest to use).
*   **The Models (The Brains):**
    *   **Small (Fits on a phone/basic laptop):** `Llama-3.2-3B` (Approx 2GB). Good for basic logic.
    *   **Medium (The Sweet Spot):** `Llama-3.1-8B` (Approx 5GB). Very smart, runs on any 8GB RAM laptop.
    *   **Heavy (Expert Level):** `Mistral-Small` or `Gemma-2-9B`.
*   **Uncensored (Crucial):** Official AIs like ChatGPT won't tell you how to make explosives or medicine. Download `Dolphin-Llama3` via Ollama for "unrestricted" information.
*   **How to get them:** Once Ollama is installed, open your command prompt and type: `ollama run llama3.1`

### 4. Massive Book Libraries (E-Books)
You need fiction for morale and textbooks for engineering.

*   **Anna’s Archive:** [annas-archive.org](https://annas-archive.org/) (The largest mirror of all books).
*   **Library Genesis (LibGen):** [libgen.is](https://libgen.is/)
    *   **What to search for:** "Mechanical Engineering," "Small Scale Farming," "Botany of Bangladesh," "Civil Engineering Handbooks."
*   **Project Gutenberg:** [gutenberg.org](https://www.gutenberg.org/)
    *   Download their "Offline Bundle" (thousands of classic books out of copyright).

### 5. Maps (Crucial for BD Navigation)
If GPS satellites stay up but the internet goes down, your phone can still navigate if you have the data.

*   **Organic Maps:** [Download for Android/iOS](https://organicmaps.app/).
*   **The Data:** Inside the app, download the **entire map of Bangladesh**. It is roughly 300MB. It includes every village road and hospital location.

### 6. Entertainment & Morale
People forget that boredom and panic kill in a crisis. 
*   **Music:** Download your favorite 500 songs in `.mp3` format (low space).
*   **Archive.org:** Download old radio plays or "Classic Cinema" that are small in file size.

---

### Important Tactical Advice for Bangladesh:

1.  **The "Humidity Kill":** In BD, the 90% humidity will corrode your electronics. Store your backup hard drives and radios in a **"Dry Box"** (a plastic airtight container with 1kg of Silica Gel).
2.  **The "Power Nap":** Buy a **12V Lead-Acid Battery** (the ones used in IPS/Easy-bikes). You can easily find these in BD. Buy a "12V to 5V USB Converter" so you can charge your devices from a car/bike battery if your solar panels are stolen or broken.
3.  **Physical Printing:** Print a "Contact Sheet" on paper. Laminate it. Include blood types, emergency meeting spots in your city, and radio frequencies. 
4.  **Download Manager:** Use **Internet Download Manager (IDM)** to download the Kiwix files. They are huge, and if your BD internet cuts out at 90%, you will lose the progress without a manager.

**Start with the "WikiMed" ZIM file and "Organic Maps." They are the highest value-per-megabyte for your family's safety.**
