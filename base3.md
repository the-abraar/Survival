For a collapse scenario in **Bangladesh**, you face specific challenges: high population density, potential grid failure, and humidity (which kills electronics).

Here is your priority list with estimated **BDT (Bangladeshi Taka)** prices and where to find them locally.

---

### Priority 1: Information Storage (The "Digital Library")
If the internet goes down, knowledge on how to treat a wound or grow food is your most valuable asset.

*   **What to buy:** 256GB or 512GB High-End MicroSD Cards (Class 10/U3) or a Portable SSD.
*   **Cost:** 2,500 BDT – 6,000 BDT.
*   **Where to buy:** [Star Tech](https://www.startech.com.bd/data-storage/micro-sd-card) or [Ryans Computers](https://www.ryanscomputers.com/).
*   **What to do:** Download the **Kiwix** ZIM files (Wikipedia, WikiMed) immediately. Store these cards in a small plastic container with silica gel to prevent fungus (common in BD).

### Priority 2: Portable Solar Power
Without electricity, your "Digital Library" is a brick. In BD, solar is the most reliable backup.

*   **What to buy:** 21W or 28W Portable Folding Solar Panel (look for brands like **Anker** or **Choetech**).
*   **Cost:** 5,000 BDT – 9,000 BDT.
*   **Where to buy:** [Daraz.com.bd](https://www.daraz.com.bd) (Search "Folding Solar Panel") or physical shops in **Dhaka Stadium Market**.
*   **Why:** This can charge your phone/power bank directly under the sun. Avoid "Solar Power Banks" (the tiny panel on the bank is useless); get the folding panels.

### Priority 3: Offline Communication (Meshtastic / LoRa)
In a war/collapse, the government or occupiers usually shut down cell towers. You need your own "texting" network.

*   **What to buy:** **LILYGO T-Beam (Meshtastic nodes)**. You need at least two (one for you, one for your family).
*   **Cost:** 4,500 BDT – 6,000 BDT per unit.
*   **Where to buy:** Hard to find in retail. You must order via **AliExpress** or find specialized hobbyist shops like **TechShopBD**.
*   **Advantage:** These use LoRa (Long Range) radio to send encrypted texts to your phone via Bluetooth. They work 5-10km apart with no towers.

### Priority 4: Short-Range Radios (Walkie Talkies)
For immediate coordination (e.g., your family is in different parts of the neighborhood).

*   **What to buy:** **Baofeng UV-5R** or **UV-82**.
*   **Cost:** 2,500 BDT – 3,500 BDT per piece.
*   **Where to buy:** [Daraz](https://www.daraz.com.bd/products/baofeng-uv-5r-dual-band-handheld-transceiver-walkie-talkie-1-pcs-i105404555.html) or shops in **Patuatuly (Old Dhaka)**.
*   **Legal Warning:** In Bangladesh, using these technically requires a BTRC license. However, in a total collapse/war scenario, they are essential for safety. 

### Priority 5: High-Capacity Power Bank
*   **What to buy:** **Baseus** or **Anker** 20,000mAh or 30,000mAh (20W+ Fast Charging).
*   **Cost:** 3,000 BDT – 5,500 BDT.
*   **Where to buy:** [Star Tech](https://www.startech.com.bd/accessories/power-bank) or any reputable gadget shop in Jamuna Future Park / Bashundhara City.

### Priority 6: Offline Maps (Zero Cost)
*   **What to do:** Download **Organic Maps** (Android/iOS). 
*   **Action:** Open the app now, search for "Bangladesh," and click **Download Map**. It includes every small "Goli" (lane) in Dhaka and village roads, working entirely offline via GPS.

### The "BD Advantage" Tactic:
In Bangladesh, the biggest threat during a collapse is **waterborne disease** and **food supply**. 
1.  **Download:** "Where There Is No Doctor" PDF and keep it on your phone.
2.  **Buy:** A **LifeStraw** or **Sawyer Mini** water filter (Approx 3,000 BDT on Daraz). In a flood or war, the tap water in Dhaka will become lethal within 48 hours. This filter will save your family.
